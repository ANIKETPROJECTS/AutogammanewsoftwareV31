---
name: Painting & Denting panel pricing
description: Panel pricing and creation rules for Painting & Denting job-card items.
---

- Standard Painting & Denting is ₹3,500 per panel; Touch-up uses a custom price.
- Whole-car Touch-up is an optional separate custom-priced item; it must not replace or alter per-panel work.
- Users can add custom panel names in the Painting & Denting job-card section. Do not move panel setup into the general service catalog.
- Each selected panel is an itemized line on the customer invoice.

**Why:** The user wants panel pricing and new panel creation handled in the dedicated job-card section, separate from the general service catalog.

**How to apply:** Preserve per-panel pricing and itemization, and treat whole-car touch-up as a separate optional invoice item with its own custom price.
