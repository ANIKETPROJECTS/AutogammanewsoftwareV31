---
name: Warranty and PPF follow-up separation
description: Product rule for distinguishing long-term warranty checkups from five-day PPF inspection reminders.
---

Keep long-term warranty checkup statuses and five-day PPF inspection reminders as separate workflows. Start the PPF five-calendar-day timer from the date a job card is marked Completed, not its creation or invoice date. Unfinished and cancelled jobs must not send reminders; preserve existing send outcomes.

**Why:** Users found the combined row and summary statuses confusing because a past-due PPF inspection could appear beside a future warranty checkup. Starting the PPF timer at recorded completion also prevents reminders from arriving before the service is finished.

**How to apply:** Keep warranty counts and filters focused on warranty checkup timing and completion. Keep PPF due dates and actual WhatsApp outcomes together in the PPF inspection area. Use a legacy date only when a completed job has no recorded completion date. Do not label a reminder Sent or Failed until a connected approved template produces a real send outcome.
