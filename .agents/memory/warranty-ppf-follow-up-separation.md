---
name: Warranty and PPF follow-up separation
description: Product rule for distinguishing long-term warranty checkups from five-day PPF inspection reminders.
---

Keep long-term warranty checkup statuses and five-day PPF inspection reminders as separate workflows. The PPF reminder due date is five calendar days after the Service Date; WhatsApp delivery state belongs with that PPF reminder, not with warranty checkup counts.

**Why:** Users found the combined row and summary statuses confusing because a past-due PPF inspection could appear beside a future warranty checkup. They approved separate tabs to make the two timelines explicit.

**How to apply:** Keep warranty counts and filters focused on warranty checkup timing and completion. Keep PPF due dates and actual WhatsApp outcomes together in the PPF inspection area. Do not label a reminder Sent or Failed until a connected approved template produces a real send outcome.
