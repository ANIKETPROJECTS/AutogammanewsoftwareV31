Simplify the cost section of the Record Purchase item row.

FIELDS PER ITEM (in this order)
1. Purchase Cost (₹/<selected unit>). This is the per-unit price paid to the supplier.
2. Sell Cost (₹/<selected unit>). This is the per-unit price charged to the customer.
3. Total Purchase Cost = Purchase Cost x Quantity (read-only, auto-calculated).
4. Total Sell Cost = Sell Cost x Quantity (read-only, auto-calculated).

CHANGES
- Remove the "Unit Cost" field from the UI completely.
- Purchase Cost now replaces Unit Cost. Supplier bill totals, GST and payments must be calculated from Purchase Cost x Quantity.
- Remove the note "Purchase Cost is for reference only and does not affect totals or payments".
- Labels must follow the selected unit (sqft, pcs, roll, ltr, kg, set, box), e.g. "Purchase Cost (₹/pcs)" and "Sell Cost (₹/pcs)". Update them instantly when the unit dropdown changes.
- Totals must recalculate live when quantity, unit, Purchase Cost or Sell Cost changes.

BOTTOM SUMMARY
- Items: count of all rows on the form (currently shows 0 even when a row exists, so fix this).
- Total Purchase Cost: sum of all item Total Purchase Costs.
- Total Sell Cost: sum of all item Total Sell Costs.
- Rename "Bill Total" to "Supplier Bill Total" = Total Purchase Cost (+ GST if External or Internal is selected).
- Remove "Reference Purchase Cost".
- The summary must count every row and update live, even if category or product is not selected yet. Make Category required on submit with a clear error message.

BILLING
- Customer billing must auto-fill the price from Sell Cost.
- Customer bill total is based on Sell Cost only, never on Purchase Cost.
- Purchase Cost must never appear on the customer-facing bill.

DATA SAFETY
- Do not delete existing saved records or the old unit_cost column.
- For old records that have a Unit Cost but a different Purchase Cost, keep the old Unit Cost value in the database and do not change their saved totals.
- New records use Purchase Cost for all supplier calculations.

TEST CASE
Quantity 4, Unit pcs, Purchase Cost 49, Sell Cost 150 should show: Total Purchase Cost ₹196, Total Sell Cost ₹600, Items 1, Supplier Bill Total ₹196 (with GST None).
